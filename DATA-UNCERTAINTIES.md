# Campus data uncertainties

These limits are product behavior, not temporary wording:

1. Founders B uses an approximate B/C building-center point, not a verified B entrance.
2. Nicarry uses an approximate building-center point.
3. Steinman uses an approximate building-center point.
4. Esbenshade is the least certain destination. The app uses a provisional Masters Center and Esbenshade-area proxy.
5. Individual classroom locations are unavailable.
6. Indoor GPS may be inaccurate.
7. The app cannot verify construction closures or temporary path changes.
8. The in-app line is straight-line orientation and does not determine a walkable path.
9. The room number must be confirmed through building signs.
10. A wrong precise claim is worse than an honest approximation.

The supplied points must not be silently replaced by geocoder output. A coordinate or marker may be upgraded only from a cited official source, with the source, change, check date, and verification limits recorded in `ROUTE-VERIFICATION.md`.

The app provides approximate building-level orientation only. It does not provide a verified walking route, entrance, indoor room navigation, floor, stairs, hallway, construction detour, or automatic rerouting. Its compass label is calculated from a straight-line bearing and is not a device compass.
