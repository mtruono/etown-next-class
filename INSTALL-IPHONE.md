# Install on iPhone

1. Open the ordinary production URL in Safari on her iPhone.
2. Confirm the Fall 2026 schedule appears immediately without a login, code, or setup link.
3. In Safari, tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
4. Open **Etown Assistant** from the Home Screen.
5. Tap **Take me to class** or **Take me home** and choose **Allow While Using App** when iOS asks for location.

The ordinary GitHub Pages URL is the only link she needs.
